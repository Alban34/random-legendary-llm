## What's in v2.2.0

---

### Fix: multiplayer game result stuck at pending status (Epic 103)

History records for games with two or more players (`playerCount ≥ 2`) were never transitioning from `pending` to `completed` after a result was saved. The root cause was a missing `playerCount` argument in the `updateGameResult` call inside `saveGameResult`. The targeted one-line fix resolves all three outcomes (win, loss, draw) for 2-player and 3-player games. Six new unit tests were added to guard against regression.

---

### Scheme setup rules applied from card review (Epic 104)

Thirteen scheme entries in the game data have been updated with structured setup modifiers and human-readable notes, sourced from scheme-card screenshots reviewed in this cycle. Schemes affected span four product lines:

**Core Set**
- `Replace Earth's Leaders with Killbots` — sets bystanders to 18
- `Secret Invasion of the Skrull Shapeshifters` — sets a flat minimum of 6 heroes (`set-min-heroes: 6`, no additional add-hero modifier); adds note that 12 heroes are shuffled into the villain deck

**Marvel Studios, Phase 1**
- `Asgard Under Siege` — adds 1 extra henchman group
- `Destroy the Cities of Earth!` — sets bystanders to 12
- `Enslave Minds with the Chitauri Scepter` — forces Chitauri villain group; adds note that 12 heroes are shuffled into the villain deck
- `Super Hero Civil War` — adds conditional modifier: 4 heroes for 2-player games
- `Replace Earth's Leaders with HYDRA` — sets bystanders to 18

**Marvel Studios' What If...?**
- `Trash Earth with Hugest Party Ever` — forces Intergalactic Party Animals villain group and requires Party Thor hero
- `Breach the Nexus of All Realities` — adds 1 extra villain group for 1–2 players
- `Marvel Zombies` — forces zombie villain group; adds note that 8 hero cards are added to the villain deck; adds note for 1–2 player conditional bystanders

**Villains**
- `Mass Produce War Machine Armor` — forces S.H.I.E.L.D. Assault Squad henchman group
- `Infiltrate the Lair with Spies` — adds note: place 21 bystanders as Infiltrating Spies next to scheme
- `Graduation at Xavier's X-Academy` — adds note: place 8 bystanders as Young Mutants next to scheme
